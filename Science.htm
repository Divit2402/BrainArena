5 Mind-Blowing Science Facts

Interactive truths about our universe

Fact #1 • Astronomy
The universe is beige on average. Scientists averaged all the light emitted by galaxies and discovered the official color of the cosmos: #FFF8E7 (Cosmic Latte).
Fact #2 • Mathematics
There are more ways to shuffle a standard 52-card deck ($52!$) than there are atoms on Earth. Every time you shuffle thoroughly, that exact sequence has likely never existed before.

Fact #3 • Physics
If you spin a subcritical sphere of uranium-235 fast enough in your hand, nothing happens. But if you bring two subcritical spheres together—reaching critical mass—city gone.
Fact #4 • Chemistry
Water can be two distinct liquids at once. When supercooled below -68°C at high pressure, it separates into two liquid phases: high-density and low-density liquid.

Genetically speaking, humans share roughly 60% of our DNA with bananas and about 90% with domestic cats due to shared cellular building blocks.

The Birthday Paradox

In a room of just 23 people, there is a roughly 50% chance that two of them share the exact same birthday. In a room of 75, that probability jumps to 99.9%.

Shuffling a Deck of Cards

There are 52! (8 × 10⁶⁷) ways to arrange a standard deck. Every time you shuffle a deck properly, it is almost certain that order has never existed in history.

Zero's Roman Nemesis

The Roman numeral system has no character or representation for zero. The concept of zero as a placeholder and number was popularized much later.

A 'Google' vs. 'Googol'

A "googol" is a 1 followed by one hundred zeros. The search engine Google was famously named after a misspelling of this massive mathematical value.

The Letter 'A'

If you write out the names of whole numbers in English (one, two, three...), the letter "a" does not appear until you reach "one thousand".

The Loneliest Even Prime

2 is the only even prime number in existence. Any other even number is divisible by 2 and therefore composite by definition.
